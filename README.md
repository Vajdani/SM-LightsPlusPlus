# Expands Lights with more features!
Twice the light strength, a light range setting, a default state setting, and the ability to paint the light and it's bulb separately!

### Important!
- This mod offers you the option of having the **modded lights override the vanilla lights**, making it so your builds have the extra features by default, and the option of having the **modded lights  as their own, separate parts**, meaning that they will not apply to your past builds.
- **By default, the modded lights are unique, and do not override vanilla lights.**
- This setting can be toggled in-game, using the **/toggleLightsOverride** chat command. You will need to **re-enter the world** for the change to take effect.
- The **drawback of the override option** is that, due to how Scrap Mechanic handles overriden parts, **lights already placed in the world will be broken**. They can be fixed by simply picking them up and putting them back down, but in warehouses for example, you wont be able to do that(since they are build protected).

- **If you and the person you're joining in multiplayer don't have the same override setting, funky things will start happening. You will see a warning in the chat when this happens.**
- **If an override mismatch does occur, just type /toggleLightsOverride in the chat, and re-join the game. After that, there should be no mismatch.**
- (Yes, I know, this is kinda annoying, but the game doesn't allow you to refresh a client's part collection on the fly)

### Features
- Applies to all lights:
  - An **extra 15 strength settings**, going from a maximum of 10 to **25**. This allows you to make much brighter lights
  - Setting whether the light should be on or off by default
  - The ability to paint the light and its emitted light colour separately
  - Connecting a **Color Block** from **The Modpack** will set the light's bulb colour
- Only applies to some lights:
  - A maximum range setting, allowing you to make lights that reach really far away
